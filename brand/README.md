# Murmur Notetaker logo

**Idea:** Levels M. Five audio-level bars whose tops draw an M: your voice becomes the letter. The
same bars are the live level meters in the app, so the logo and the product speak one language.

## Type

Wordmark: Geist, weight 650, tracking -2 %, converted to outlines (no font needed). App UI: Geist /
Geist Mono (SIL Open Font License).

## Rules

- **Clear space:** at least the width of one bar (28 units on the 256 grid) on every side.
- **Minimum size:** symbol 16 px (use the small cut below 32 px); lockup 120 px wide.
- **Lockup:** bars stand on the wordmark baseline and rise above the cap height; keep the gap
  (about one bar and a half) and the 0.62 ratio. Don't rebuild it from parts.
- **Don't:** recolour single bars, add gradients or shadows, change the bar heights, set the name in
  another font, rotate, or put the blue symbol on a busy photo (use the white version on a scrim).
